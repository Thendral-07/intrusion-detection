Jupyter Notebook ML Lifecycle (12 Steps)

cyber_security_step_by_step.ipynb
STEP 1 - 3 Zero-Leakage Stratified Ingestion Verified
9,537 session logs split into 80% Train (7,629) and 20% Test (1,908) stratified by attack_detected. Scaler and imputers fitted strictly on X_train with zero data leakage.
STEP 4 - 6 Robust Scaler & Categorical Encoders Fitted
Imputed missing crypto (SimpleImputer(fill_value='Unknown')), median imputed numeric features, standardized continuous variables with StandardScaler, and encoded categorical variables into 17 feature dimensions.
STEP 7 - 8 RandomForestClassifier Training AUC: 0.9032
Trained 300 decision trees with class_weight='balanced_subsample'. Evaluated on unseen test set yielding 0.9032 ROC-AUC, 85.48% Accuracy, 88.75% Recall, and 81.20% Precision.
STEP 9 - 12 Calibrated Threshold & Production Bundle Threshold = 0.35
Calibrated custom security threshold to 0.35 for optimal zero-trust high-threat recall. Exported complete model + scaler + encoder bundle to intrusion_model_bundle.joblib for production runtime serving.
